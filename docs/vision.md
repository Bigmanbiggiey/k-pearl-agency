# K Pearl Agency — Product Vision

## Vision

Create a trusted, premium digital home for K Pearl Agency where people can discover quality properties and connect with the agency quickly.

## Mission

Use clear property information, strong photography, straightforward enquiry flows and a premium brand experience to make property discovery and agency engagement easier.

## Product positioning

K Pearl should be perceived as:
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Premium
- Local and knowledgeable
- Responsive
- Transparent

## Business model

> **Phase 0 note (2026-08-27):** `docs/product-definition.md` §2 recommends
> **Model B — a sales + rental agency catalogue**, with the remaining services
> (marketing, sourcing, landlord representation, management, valuation) presented as
> content + lead capture rather than software workflows. Pending business approval.

The website primarily supports an agency business rather than acting as an open peer-to-peer marketplace.

Potential agency services:
- Property marketing
- Property letting/rental support
- Property sales
- Property sourcing
- Landlord/owner representation
- Property management, if offered by the business

The final service list must be confirmed with K Pearl before implementation.

## MVP success

A visitor should be able to:
1. Understand what K Pearl does.
2. Browse properties.
3. Filter/search properties.
4. Open a property detail page.
5. Contact K Pearl about a property.
6. Request a viewing or callback.
7. Contact the agency for general services.

A staff member should be able to:
1. Sign in.
2. Add/edit/archive properties.
3. Upload/manage property images.
4. Mark properties featured/verified.
5. Review and manage enquiries.
