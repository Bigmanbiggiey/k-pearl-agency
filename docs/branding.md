# K Pearl Agency — Branding

## Official identity supplied for the project

Brand name:
**K.pearl Agency**

The logo also presents:
**AGENCY**

Logo tagline:
**MARKETING REAL ESTATE, CREATING VALUE**

## Visual identity

The supplied artwork uses:
- black background
- gold metallic typography
- gold real-estate line art
- black pearl motif
- elegant serif-style wordmark

## Digital adaptation

For the website:
- Preserve the supplied logo without recoloring it.
- Use a clean light content surface where it improves readability, while retaining black/gold as the brand system.
- A dark hero can use the original logo naturally.
- Avoid recreating the logo with CSS.
- Store official assets under `frontend/public/assets/branding/`.

## Brand tone

Copy should be:
- confident
- professional
- concise
- service-oriented
- trustworthy
- premium without sounding exaggerated

Avoid:
- fake claims such as “#1 in Kenya” unless verified
- exaggerated guarantees
- generic AI-generated corporate filler
